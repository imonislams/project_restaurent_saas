Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers for restaurant owners and operations directors managing daily cash volume.

How does blind drop work if a server is legitimately short?

expand_more
If a server completes their blind count and the backend variance exceeds the restaurant's tolerance limit (e.g. ±$5.00), the terminal flags the shift without revealing the exact shortfall amount. A manager must swipe their NFC supervisor badge or enter a PIN to open the discrepancy audit screen, inspect itemized sales chits, and sign off with a mandatory incident note.

Can managers disburse cash for emergency supplies without throwing off the drawer balance?

expand_more
Yes. The 'Paid-Out' workflow allows quick cash removal while automatically deducting that amount from the drawer's expected close balance. The terminal's camera snaps the paper vendor receipt, runs OCR on the receipt line items, and instantly routes the expense into the appropriate GL expense category (e.g., Food Supplies, CO2, Bar Produce).

How are credit card tips paid out in cash vs payroll without creating tax liabilities?

expand_more
Mise lets you select tip payout methodology per employee tier: Daily Cash Payout, Direct-to-Debit Card (TipHaus integration), or Next-Cycle Payroll. When CC tips are settled with drawer cash at midnight, Mise issues a digital tip ledger receipt to the server and creates an automated offsetting entry in your payroll clearing account, ensuring tip reporting matches IRS Form 8027 requirements.

Does Mise support multiple cash drawers per terminal or floating cashiers?

expand_more
Yes. Dual-cash-drawer configurations allow two bartenders to share a single terminal while maintaining completely isolated, lock-assigned cash floats. We also support 'Floating Server Banks,' where each server carries their own cash apron pouch, checking in and checking out against the central house safe independently.
