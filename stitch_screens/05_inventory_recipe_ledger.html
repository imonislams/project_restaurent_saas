KITCHEN REALITIES

Frequently Asked by Executive Chefs & F&B Directors

Real kitchens are chaotic, hot, and constantly shifting. Here is how Mise accounts for the reality of culinary operations.

How does Mise handle daily yield variations on whole butchery or whole fish breakdown? expand_more

Mise includes a dedicated Whole-Animal Butchery Ledger. When your brigade breaks down a 45kg whole Halibut or whole side of beef, you enter the raw weight and resulting yields (fillets, collar, trim for tartare, bones for stock). Mise dynamically calculates the accurate cost per portion for each yield stream based on your actual cut weights that morning, rather than a theoretical textbook ratio.

Can we take physical inventory counts on mobile tablets without halting service? expand_more

Yes. Mise supports live concurrent counting on iOS and Android handhelds. Multiple sous chefs can count the walk-in, line coolers, and cellar simultaneously. Mise stamps each count timestamp against tickets fired in real-time, accurately reconciling in-flight orders without requiring kitchen shut-downs.

What happens when a chef substitutes an ingredient on the fly during service? expand_more

Using the Line Lead POS quick-action panel, chefs can trigger a “Service Substitution” in two taps (e.g., “Sub Chanterelles for Morels on Risotto for remainder of service”). Mise instantly re-routes depletion from Morel bins to Chanterelle bins and recalibrates dish margin for that shift’s run.

Does Mise support multiple stock locations like dry storage, cellar, and walk-in? expand_more

Yes. Every restaurant floor plan in Mise can map distinct inventory locations: Main Walk-In, Pastry Freezer, Dry Storage, Bar Cellar, Line Low-Boys, and Satellite Service Bars. Internal transfers between locations are logged with a quick tap, giving multi-room venues precise stock visibility.
