Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for floor managers, beverage directors, and hospitality directors.

What happens if a server drops a handheld into water or soup?

All certified commercial terminals feature IP65 sealed casings that resist liquid spills and grease. If a device ever sustains critical hardware damage mid-service, our 10-second instant hardware swap protocol allows any server to grab a spare unit from the charging cradle, enter their 4-digit PIN, and resume their exact open table state without losing a single item.

Does tableside payment feel awkward or rushed for fine dining?

Not at all. Mise provides customizable presentation folios and privacy tipping screens. For white-tablecloth environments, servers can present the terminal in an artisan leather bill folio or offer an instant tableside QR code on a discreet card so guests can pay and tip on their own phones while lingering over espresso.

How long does handheld battery life last during a double shift?

Our optimized low-power UI delivers 12 to 14 hours of continuous screen-on time on commercial devices. For operations running 16-hour weekend brunch-to-dinner shifts, our multi-bay charging docks support 3-second magnetic hot-swappable batteries that maintain screen state without restarting the device.

Can servers send orders directly from the patio if the main internet is down?

Yes. Mise Mesh operates on a local-first peer architecture. Handhelds communicate directly with kitchen printers and KDS units across the local Wi-Fi router or sub-GHz mesh radio. Even if your external fiber ISP goes down completely, orders fire to the line and receipts print locally without internet access.
