Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if our restaurant internet cuts out during Friday dinner rush?

Mise operates on an offline-first local mesh architecture. Even if your ISP drops completely, terminals communicate directly with kitchen printers and KDS screens over your local router. Orders fire, checks print, and offline card taps store safely in encrypted storage, automatically re-syncing to the cloud once connectivity resumes.

How fast can we migrate our existing menu and nested modifier groups?

Our concierge culinary team handles full menu ingestion within 24 hours. Simply email us your PDF menu or export from your previous POS (Toast, Square, Micros, or Lightspeed). We map all coursing flags, modifier groups, and allergen tags so you can launch immediately.

Can we test Mise alongside our current POS without breaking operations?

Yes. We frequently set up parallel pilot trials for dining rooms. You can run Mise on a single patio station or test our Kitchen Display System alongside your existing setup during our 14-day zero-risk trial period.
