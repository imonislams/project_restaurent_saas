OPERATOR COMMON QUESTIONS

Built for the realities of the floor.

What happens if our internet drops during an 8:00 PM Friday rush? wifi_off

Nothing stops. Terminals immediately transition to local mesh mode over your internal router without showing so much as a spinner. Table orders continue dispatching directly to kitchen printers and KDS units. Card transactions queue securely in encrypted offline storage and process the millisecond internet connection is restored.

Can our servers split a 10-person check across multiple credit cards, Apple Pay, and cash? call_split

Yes, flawlessly. Mise supports split-by-seat, fractional multi-guest splits (e.g., splitting expensive cellar bottles across 4 guests while keeping entrees isolated), and mixed tender types on individual guest bills with zero balancing errors.

How difficult is it to migrate our existing 300-item menu and nested modifiers? sync_alt

Our automated migration engine parses exports from Toast, Micros, Aloha, Lightspeed, and Clover in under 4 minutes. Our culinary onboarding team manually verifies nested recipes, allergen flags, and station routing chits within 24 hours at zero onboarding cost.

Does Mise support mobile handheld tablets for patio and table-side ordering? devices

Yes. The exact same sub-100ms interface scales responsively to iPad Mini or 6-inch Android handhelds (such as WisePOS E). Servers can fire drink orders right from the patio table while the bar printer chits before they even step back into the dining room.
