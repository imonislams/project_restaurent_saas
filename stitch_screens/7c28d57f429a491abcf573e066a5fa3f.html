Executive Inquiries

Multi-unit operations, answered.

How does Mise handle different legal entities, sales taxes, and bank payouts for each branch? expand_more

Each location can be assigned an isolated Merchant Account, distinct Employer Identification Number (EIN), state/municipal tax schedule, and bank routing configuration. The parent corporate entity retains global ledger visibility while each restaurant functions legally and financially as an autonomous operating subsidiary.

Can individual branch GMs make emergency menu 86s or price adjustments without corporate approval? expand_more

Yes. Real-time ingredient 86ing is always enabled at the local POS terminal for service safety and guest satisfaction. Price adjustments, however, are role-permissioned: you can grant branch GMs discretionary variance bounds (e.g., ±$2.00) or enforce strict corporate approval locks that require Regional Director sign-off via push notification.

How do central commissary prep yields reflect in each restaurant's individual P&L? expand_more

When commissary prep batches are completed and verified through digital barcode manifests, the raw cost is tracked against the commissary ledger. Upon store-level receiving handshake, internal transfer billing debits the receiving location’s COGS at negotiated internal transfer prices and credits the commissary hub.

Can we migrate our multi-unit portfolio from Toast, NCR Aloha, or Micros without dining room downtime? expand_more

Yes. Our Enterprise Culinary Solutions team executes staggered cutovers. We ingest existing menu matrices, historical sales databases, and floor layouts in a staging environment. Transition takes place post-service during normal kitchen turnover hours with zero impact on active guest dining shifts.
